OUR FOUNDER

Meet Shailendra Vijay

The visionary who left his job to build futures

Founder & Director

Shailendra Vijay

M.Sc. in Electronics | Founder, ZSVTC

Shailendra Vijay is a passionate educationist who sacrificed his government job (Sub-Auditor) in 1996 to dedicate his life to preparing young aspirants for competitive examinations.

Under his guidance, students from small towns have secured prestigious positions in Banking Services, IPS, IAS pathways, and various central & state government departments. His students have made their mark across India.

With a teaching philosophy rooted in conceptual clarity, disciplined practice, and personal mentoring, he continues to inspire thousands of students to achieve their dreams of serving the nation through government service.
